## 1. Character Illustrations & Artwork
- **Type**: Detailed full-color cel-shaded character illustrations with transparent background cutouts.
- **Characters**: 
  - Ichigo Kurosaki (Shinigami robes with Zangetsu)
  - Rukia Kuchiki (Sode no Shirayuki with flowing ribbon)
  - Orihime Inoue (Karakura uniform with Shun Shun Rikka hairpins)
  - Yasutora "Chad" Sado (Brazo Derecha de Gigante armored arm)
  - Uryu Ishida (Quincy uniform with spirit bow Kojaku)
  - Renji Abarai (Zabimaru whip-blade & tattoos)
  - Byakuya Kuchiki (Captain haori with Senbonzakura cherry blossoms)
  - Toshiro Hitsugaya (Captain haori with Daiguren Hyorinmaru ice wings)
- **Format**: High-resolution transparent PNG assets in `public/assets/*.png`.
- **License**: Non-commercial fan tribute artwork inspired by Tite Kubo's manga and anime character designs.
